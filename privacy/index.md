This is a personal website. It shows no ads, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and does not ask for or store any personal information.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Comments

Blog posts use [Disqus](https://disqus.com) for comments. The Disqus widget loads from Disqus's servers and is subject to the [Disqus privacy policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).

## Third-party content

Some pages load scripts or styles from public CDNs (for example jQuery, Bootstrap and MathJax), so those providers receive your IP address when you visit.
