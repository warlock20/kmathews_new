<b>"Ctrl + space" </b> or content assisting is not working in eclipse!! Well, all you want to do is, enable it :-).
<p> In this example, i am enabling the content assisting for cpp editor. Go to  <b> Window -> Preferences -> C++ -> Editor -> Content Assist -> Advanced </b>. In this menu, you can enable different kinds of proposals or assistings.</p>
